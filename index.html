Your Mac is the server. That's the whole idea.

Raza Photos and Raza Songs turn the photo and music folders on your Mac into a library that every iPhone and iPad in your house can browse and play. No cloud, no account, no subscription. Your devices find the Mac automatically over Bonjour, and your files never move.

Privacy is architectural, not a promise. The apps make no outbound requests and have no telemetry, so there is no server for your data to reach.

Both are native Swift apps, with HDR, Live Photos, on-device face recognition, gapless audio, CarPlay and AirPlay. Edits you make from your phone write back into the original files as open XMP/EXIF and audio tags, so nothing is locked in and you can leave any time.

Have more than one Mac? Their libraries merge into one. Access is controlled per device with read-only toggles, and remote access is optional, through encrypted, invite-only Tailscale.
