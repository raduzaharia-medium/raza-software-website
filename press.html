In one sentence

Raza Photos and Raza Songs turn the photo and music folders on your Mac into a library that every iPhone and iPad in the house can browse and play. There is no cloud, no account, and no subscription.

About fifty words

Raza Photos and Raza Songs are native apps for Mac, iPhone and iPad. Your Mac is the server: point an app at your folders and your devices find the library on your home network automatically. Nothing is uploaded, there are no accounts, and the apps have no telemetry. It's the convenience of a cloud service without the cloud, built so a household doesn't have to run a server.

Key facts

Platforms: macOS 26, iOS 26 and iPadOS 26 or later. Price: free for now, and it will be a one-time purchase, never a subscription. Privacy: no accounts, no telemetry, and the library never goes to any server. Raza Photos uses Apple Maps for map tiles and place names. Built in: the EU, in native Swift. Open source: no. Languages: Raza Photos is available in English, French, German, Spanish and Italian.
