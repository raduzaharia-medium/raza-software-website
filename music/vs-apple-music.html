Not really competitors

Raza Songs is a player for music you already own, served from your Mac. Apple Music is a subscription to a streaming catalog. The question isn't which is better. It's which one you're missing.

When Apple Music is the better choice

You want to discover new music, explore a huge catalog, or listen away from home without keeping a Mac on. Raza Songs doesn't replace that and doesn't try to.

When Raza Songs is

You've built a collection over years, from ripped CDs and purchases and downloads, and you'd like it on every device in the house without a subscription standing between you and your own music. It's also the choice if you'd rather your music keep playing whatever happens to a service.
