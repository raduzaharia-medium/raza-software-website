Shuffle lives on the queue, not the transport controls.

Because the queue is what it changes. Most apps make shuffle a toggle sitting next to play and pause — flip it on, and it quietly reorders everything, including songs you've already heard, and the track you're mid-listen to. Ours works differently.

Tap shuffle and it's a single, one-time reshuffle of what comes next — not a setting you leave on. Everything still to come in the queue gets randomized. The song currently playing keeps playing, uninterrupted. Everything already played stays exactly where it was. You never lose your place, and you never wonder whether shuffle is "on."

Playback that just works.

The details you don't think about until they're missing — tracks that flow into each other, and playback that survives a phone call.

The next track is buffered and ready before the current one ends, so albums meant to flow together — live sets, concept records — play without a beat of silence between songs.

A call or another app's audio pauses playback — and resumes automatically once the system signals it's safe to. Lock screen, Control Center, and CarPlay all get full transport controls, artwork, and track info.

Repeat is one tap away from the mini player wherever you are in the app — no need to jump into Now Playing just to loop a track.
