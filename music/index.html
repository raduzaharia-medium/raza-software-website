How it works

Three steps.
Then you're done forever.

No maintenance, no server to update, no VPN to configure — the same local-first approach as Raza Photos, our Mac and iPhone photo app, just built for your music library instead.

Point Raza Songs at your music folders. It indexes the metadata and leaves every file exactly where it is — nothing moves, nothing is copied.

Using Bonjour, your Mac quietly announces the library on your local network. No ports to open. No router settings. Nothing to configure. How does that work? →

On any iPhone, iPad, or other Mac in the house, open Raza Songs. The library is just there. If you have multiple Macs, all libraries appear merged into one.
