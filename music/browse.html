Every track shown as its own album art, with the title and artist underneath and the running length in the corner. Scroll through your library the way a record shelf works — by cover, not by text.

  • Instant thumbnail loading over Wi-Fi
  • Filter by artist, album, genre, language, or release year from the sidebar — each choice narrows the others
  • Tap any track to jump into Now Playing
  • Switch to list anytime, same library and filters underneath

Switch to list and the same library becomes rows — title, artist, and length in a compact line. When you already know what you're looking for, list gets you there faster than artwork ever could.

  • Compact rows: title, artist, and length
  • Same filters and search as grid
  • Same tap-to-play behavior
  • Multiple libraries appear merged into one
  • Build playlists with drag-and-drop ordering — shared with everyone in the house, not just tied to your device

A playlist is compiled from whichever Macs are on the network at the time — each contributes the songs it has, in order. If one Mac is asleep or off the network, its songs are simply skipped rather than breaking the playlist; the rest plays in the order you set. Bring that Mac back online and its tracks slot back in automatically.
