Version 3

3.4

  • Reconnects reliably when you return to the app from the background.
  • Smoother, steadier connections between your devices.
  • Tidier sidebar spacing.

3.3

  • Album art upscaling is now a setting.
  • Fixed a flickering connections list.
  • Switching between connections is more dependable.

3.2

  • Better integration with the system's Now Playing controls.
  • Deleted items now go to the Trash instead of being removed permanently.
  • Stability and import fixes.

3.1

  • Face ID app lock keeps your library private.
  • Fixed duplicate artists, genres, languages and other entries that differed only by upper or lower case.

3.0

  • Remote access with Tailscale: reach your music library away from home with "Share over Tailscale". It is optional, and off by default.
  • Playlist import and export.
  • Faster, more reliable connections to your library.
  • Better translations.

Version 2

2.1

  • Raza Music is now Raza Songs.
  • Better translations.

2.0

  • AirPlay support.
  • Renaming an album, artist, genre, language or playlist now merges it with an existing one of the same name.
  • A notice appears when you reach the selection limit.
  • Localization fixes.

Version 1

1.9

  • Fixed search leaving out songs when filtering, and daily highlights not refreshing.
  • Fixed the sidebar not switching to the queue on Mac, and a missing sidebar on launch.
  • Small polish to the Playing overlay.

1.8

  • Fixed navigation, queue search and song search.
  • Faster, more reliable updates between your devices.

1.7

  • Songs can now have a language.
  • Better sorting everywhere.
  • Album of the Day now plays from track 1.
  • Fixed adding a highlighted artist or album to the queue adding only one song.
  • Fixed duplicate songs in the daily selection.

1.6

  • Song filtering, plus library health filters.
  • Filter by year added.
  • Search within the sidebar filters.
  • Translations.

1.5

  • Fixed music stopping shortly after the app goes to the background.
  • Fixed missing artwork and playback failures for highlights served by another device.

1.4

  • Playlists: create, rename, reorder and delete, kept up to date across your devices.
  • Albums are sorted by disc and track number.
  • New icons for missing artwork.
  • The daily selection grows from 10 to 20 songs.
  • Translations and layout fixes.

1.3

  • Gapless playback.
  • Better handling when playback is interrupted in the background.
  • Improved shuffle and queue.
  • Album art in song lists.
  • Better M4A support.

1.2

  • A custom sidebar with drag and drop and selection.
  • Faster, smoother lists and grids on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  • Collapsible sections, with selected items shown first.
  • Improved daily highlights.
  • The current song now pauses cleanly before the next one starts.

1.1

  • Shuffle and repeat modes.
  • The app opens on the Playing tab with daily highlights and a collapsed queue.
  • Redesigned Now Playing, now a tab on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  • Edit album, artist, artwork and year, and delete from your library and disk.
  • Better selection mode, filtering and grid.
  • Faster, more flexible connections between devices.
  • The Mac app no longer launches at login by default.

1.0

  • First release: import your music and browse it in a grid.
  • Edit artist, genre, album and release year.
  • A mini player and a full Now Playing screen with a seek bar, designed for iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  • Album art comes from your own library.
  • Selection mode and filtering.
