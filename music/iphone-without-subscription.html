Option 1: Apple's Finder sync

Apple's own way is to copy your music onto the phone. Connect the iPhone to the Mac with a cable, select it in the Finder sidebar, open the Music tab, turn on Sync music onto your iPhone, choose your whole library or a selection, and click Apply. After the first sync you can also switch on syncing over Wi-Fi from the device's General tab.

The music then lives on the phone, so it plays anywhere, but it uses phone storage and the phone is updated to match your Mac. If you subscribe to Apple Music, your library is managed through iCloud Music Library instead, and Finder's music sync is usually unavailable. Sync versus streaming, explained →
