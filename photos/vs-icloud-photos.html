Two answers to the same problem

iCloud Photos keeps your library on Apple's servers and syncs it to your devices. Raza Photos leaves your library on your Mac and streams it to your devices at home. Neither is wrong. They trade different things.

Where iCloud Photos is the better choice

You want an automatic offsite copy without thinking about it. You want your library reachable from anywhere with no setup. You rely on Shared Albums or iCloud links. You don't want to keep a Mac switched on. In those cases iCloud Photos does something Raza Photos deliberately doesn't.

Where Raza Photos is

You don't want to pay every month to keep access to your own photos. You'd rather your library never sit on someone else's servers. You want the files in plain folders and open formats. You have a Mac that is usually on, and a home network you trust. And you're fine with backups being your job.

You don't have to choose today

Many people start with both. Use Transfer to copy your library to your Mac, keep iCloud Photos on while you set up a backup, and decide later whether to cancel. If you do, the steps are in Cancel iCloud storage without losing your photos.
