Version 3

3.4

  • Reconnects reliably when you return to the app from the background.
  • Smoother, steadier connections between your devices.
  • Tidier sidebar spacing.

3.3

  • Fixed photo swiping after zooming in, and a flickering connections list.
  • Switching between connections is more dependable.
  • Photos look sharper when enlarged, thanks to better upscaling.

3.2

  • Images and thumbnails load faster and more smoothly.
  • Place names are more accurate, using Apple Maps location data.
  • Deleted items now go to the Trash instead of being removed permanently.
  • Stability and import fixes.

3.1

  • Face ID app lock keeps your library private.
  • Simpler, easier video player controls.
  • Fixed duplicate people, places and other entries that differed only by upper or lower case.

3.0

  • Remote access with Tailscale: reach your library away from home with "Share over Tailscale". It is optional, and off by default.
  • Faster, more reliable connections to your library.
  • Better translations.
